Frequently Asked Questions

What is USB Slideshow & Signage? +

It is a Roku app for playing USB photos, videos, and optional music as a full-screen slideshow or signage loop.

Do I need internet for local USB playback? +

Local USB playback does not require a Service Provider media server. Internet may still be needed for Roku subscriptions, Help/FAQ, legal pages, support, and feedback links.

Which file types are recognized? +

The app recognizes many common image, video, and audio extensions. Actual playback depends on Roku platform codec, container, device, and file support.

Can I choose slide timing? +

Yes. The Home Menu includes fixed slide interval options such as 3, 5, 7, 10, 12, 15, and 20 seconds.

Can I shuffle slides? +

Yes. Shuffle Slides can be toggled from the Home Menu and applies to Home Menu Start Slideshow visual order.

Does the app support digital signage? +

Yes. It is designed for simple USB-based slideshow and signage loops. Test your media before public display and keep backup copies of important files.

Can I schedule playlists? +

No. The current app focuses on USB browsing and slideshow/signage playback. It does not provide time/date scheduling or cloud playlist management.

Where can I manage subscription information? +

The app includes Subscribe and Manage Subscription options. Purchases, renewals, and entitlement recovery are handled through Roku.
