Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the FolderCast application for Roku devices and Roku TV devices (the "Application"). The Application is provided by Deepak PK (the "Service Provider") as a local-network media utility for viewing images and playing supported audio and video selected in a browser on your phone, tablet, or computer.

Summary

The Application connects your Roku with media that you explicitly select in a browser on another device on the same local network. The Service Provider does not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, or similar analytics or tracking services in the Roku version of the Application.

Information the Application Handles

To browse and play your selected media, the Application may handle:

- Selected folder names, file names, relative paths, file types, sizes, and available media metadata
- Thumbnails, rendered images, subtitle files, and audio or video data needed for your current viewing or playback action
- Local device addresses, browser identifiers, device labels, connection state, and viewing or playback state
- Roku subscription product identifiers, purchase identifiers, entitlement status, and related billing status provided by Roku

This information is used for local connection, browsing, playback, recovery, and subscription access control. Your browser may retain a local identifier and device label, and the Roku may retain subscription state in its application storage.

Information the Application Does Not Collect for Tracking

The Roku version of the Application does not use the Application to track your activity across apps or websites. The Service Provider does not use the Application to collect analytics events, advertising identifiers, precise location information, or behavioral tracking information for the purpose of profiling or targeted advertising.

Media Sharing and Local Processing

You choose which files or folders to share. The browser reads the selected media and sends metadata, previews, images, subtitles, or playback data to your Roku over the local network as needed. Images may be rendered in the browser for display on the TV. The Service Provider does not operate a media-upload service for this local sharing flow and does not upload your selected media library to an analytics or marketing platform.

Third-Party Services

The release version does not include Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google Play Services. Roku provides the platform and payment services and may process account, purchase, and device information under its own policies. The Application retrieves Help, Terms, and Privacy content from the Service Provider's website. Opening support or feedback links may involve the linked provider's own data practices.

A developer diagnostic build may send diagnostic logs to a temporary collector on the developer's computer. Those logs can include file names, media metadata, device information, and errors. The normal release build does not enable this diagnostic collector.

Data Retention

The browser retains selected-file access and related metadata for the sharing session. Roku temporarily caches previews and rendered images. Clear Temporary Storage in Settings deletes managed temporary files; needed thumbnails can be regenerated. Removing a share or ending a session can release its active resources, but browser storage, Roku subscription state, and platform caches can have separate lifetimes. The Service Provider does not maintain a cloud-hosted media library for this local sharing feature.

Children's Privacy

The Service Provider does not knowingly use the Application to solicit personal information from children.

Security

The Service Provider takes reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information in the Roku version of the Application. Use trusted media and a private local network. The local sharing connection is not intended to be exposed to the public internet. Local network traffic is not protected by HTTPS, and access to your network and devices should be controlled.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If that happens, the updated version will be posted at the current Privacy Policy page.
