Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the USB Slideshow & Signage application for Roku devices and Roku TV devices (the "Application"). The Application is provided by Deepak PK (the "Service Provider") as a USB slideshow and digital signage utility for playing locally stored photos, videos, and optional audio from a USB drive on a TV.

Summary

USB Slideshow & Signage is designed primarily for local USB media playback on your Roku device. The Service Provider does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Play Services, or similar third-party analytics or advertising tracking services in the Roku version of the Application.

Information the Application Handles

To provide the slideshow, signage, gallery, settings, subscription, support, and legal-page features, the Application may handle information such as:

  • Media files you choose to place on a USB drive and access with the Application, including photos, videos, and audio files
  • File and folder details available from the USB drive, such as file names, folder names, file paths, extensions, media type classification, thumbnails or placeholders, and related metadata when available
  • Playback and display preferences, such as slide interval, shuffle setting, selected background music, image display mode, background color, and gallery or playback state
  • Device and app settings information used inside the Application, such as display resolution, aspect ratio, subscription state, entitlement recovery state, app version, and temporary storage information
  • Temporary app data used to support USB scanning, gallery display, thumbnails, playback, background audio selection, subscriptions, help pages, legal pages, support pages, performance, and error recovery
  • Subscription, purchase, entitlement, catalog, recovery, renewal, expiration, or billing-status information made available through the Roku platform
  • Support or feedback information you choose to send through external support pages, feedback forms, email, or other linked resources

This information is used to provide USB media discovery, gallery browsing, slideshow and signage playback, background music, saved settings, free-mode limits, subscription access control, support, troubleshooting, and related in-app features.

Local USB Media

The Application is designed to read supported media from USB storage connected to your Roku device. The Roku version of the Application is not designed to upload your photos, videos, audio files, folders, or media library to a Service Provider cloud storage account.

Media remains on the USB drive unless the Roku platform or the Application temporarily caches data needed for normal operation, such as thumbnails, display state, or playback-related data. You are responsible for the contents of the USB drive and for removing any private or sensitive files you do not want accessible on your TV.

USB Scanning and Playback

The Application may scan folders and subfolders on a connected USB drive to find supported image, video, and audio file extensions. Classification is based on file information such as names and extensions, and actual playback depends on Roku platform support, file condition, codec/container support, device resources, and app behavior.
