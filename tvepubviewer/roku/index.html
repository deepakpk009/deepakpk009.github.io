Frequently Asked Questions

What is TV EPUB Viewer? +

TV EPUB Viewer is a Roku app for reading EPUB books on your TV with a remote-friendly interface.

How do I open EPUB books on my Roku TV? +

Open the Connect screen, scan the QR code or enter the short address from a phone, tablet, or computer on the same Wi-Fi network, then choose the EPUB files you want to read.

Which book format is supported? +

The app is designed for EPUB books.

Can I search inside a book? +

Yes. The reader includes EPUB text search with results that can jump to matching pages.

Can I choose more than one EPUB? +

Yes. You can select multiple EPUB files, and folder selection may also be available on supported devices and browsers.

Do my device and Roku need to be on the same Wi-Fi network? +

Yes. The phone, tablet, or computer used to choose EPUB books should be connected to the same local Wi-Fi network as your Roku device.

Does the app remember where I stopped? +

Yes. The reader can reopen an EPUB with the last page and reading settings such as text size, background color, and night mode.

Can I change the reading view? +

Yes. The options overlay includes text zoom, background color, night mode, thumbnails, search, Go to Page, and close controls.

Does Roku store my original EPUB files? +

No. TV EPUB Viewer is designed for local reading and does not persist original EPUB files on Roku. Temporary viewing data can be cleared from Settings.

Where can I manage subscription information? +

The app includes Subscribe and Manage Subscription options. Subscription purchase and recovery use the Roku channel store flow.
