Frequently Asked Questions

What is TV PPTX Viewer? +

TV PPTX Viewer is a Roku app for viewing PowerPoint presentations on your TV with a remote-friendly interface.

How do I open PPTX files on my Roku TV? +

Open the Connect screen, scan the QR code or enter the short address from a phone, tablet, or computer on the same Wi-Fi network, then choose the PPTX files you want to view.

Which presentation format is supported? +

The app is designed for PPTX presentation files.

Can I choose more than one PPTX file? +

Yes. You can select multiple PPTX files, and folder selection may also be available on supported devices and browsers.

Do my device and Roku need to be on the same Wi-Fi network? +

Yes. The phone, tablet, or computer used to choose PPTX files should be connected to the same local Wi-Fi network as your Roku device.

Can I search inside a presentation? +

Yes. The viewer includes search with results that can jump to matching slides when supported by the presentation.

Can I zoom, rotate, or pan slides? +

Yes. The presentation options menu includes zoom, reset zoom, rotate, and pan support for zoomed slides.

Can I jump to a specific slide? +

Yes. Use Go to Slide from the presentation options menu and enter a slide number with the TV-friendly keypad.

Does the app remember where I stopped? +

While you are using the app, the viewer can restore per-presentation state such as slide position, zoom, rotation, pan, Night Mode, and Animation Mode.

Does Roku store my original PPTX files? +

No. TV PPTX Viewer is designed for local viewing and is not designed to permanently store original PPTX files on Roku. Temporary viewing data can be cleared from Settings.

Where can I manage subscription information? +

The app includes Subscribe and Manage Subscription options. Subscription purchase and recovery use the Roku channel store flow.
