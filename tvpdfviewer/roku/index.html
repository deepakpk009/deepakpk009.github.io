Frequently Asked Questions

What is TV PDF Viewer? +

TV PDF Viewer is a Roku app for reading PDF documents on your TV using a remote-friendly interface.

How can I open PDFs on my Roku TV? +

Open the Connect screen, scan the QR code or enter the shown address on a phone, tablet, or computer on the same Wi-Fi network, then choose the PDF files you want to view.

Do my device and Roku need to be on the same Wi-Fi network? +

Yes. The phone, tablet, or computer you use to choose PDFs should be connected to the same local Wi-Fi network as your Roku device.

Can I choose more than one PDF? +

Yes. You can select multiple PDF files, and folder selection may also be available on supported devices.

Can I view password-protected PDFs? +

Yes. If a PDF requires a password, you can unlock it from the device you are using to choose the file.

Can I search inside PDFs? +

Yes. The viewer includes PDF text search with result snippets and page numbers for supported documents.

Does the app remember where I stopped? +

Yes. The viewer can reopen a PDF with the last viewed page and viewing settings such as zoom, fit mode, rotation, background color, and night mode.

Can I zoom, rotate, or change the reading view? +

Yes. The options overlay includes zoom, fit mode, rotate, background color, night mode, search, thumbnails, and Go to Page.

Does Roku store my full PDF files? +

TV PDF Viewer is designed for local viewing. The app keeps temporary viewing data for active documents and includes a Settings option to clear temporary storage.

Where can I manage subscription information? +

The app includes a subscribe page and a manage subscription page with information about managing your subscription.
